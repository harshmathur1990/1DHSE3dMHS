\section{Flow}
\code{column\_hse\_tau} (aligned, gauged; see the \code{column-hse-ltau} notes) gives $u=\ln P$ and $z_0$ on all levels; a failed
column ends the solve with \code{:eos\_out\_of\_envelope} (exit 3, no output). With \code{start = :previous} (embedding) the solved
region starts from the workspace's last valid solution instead; the objective's weights then come from that start, so a warm and
a cold solve of the same cube minimise slightly different objectives. \code{solve\_tau} solves for $u$ on levels $k>k_c$ and
$c=z_{k_c}$ per column, with $u_{k\le k_c}$ and $z_k-z_{k_c}$ ($k<k_c$) fixed. Then \code{gauge!} (mean $z(\tau{=}1)=0$),
\code{tau\_state!} ($\rho$, $n_e$, $\chi$ per node), \code{check\_fields!} (as in \code{column-hse-ltau}), and the output in the
\code{column-hse-ltau} layout with the status attributes (Sect.~\ref{sec:stop}).

\textbf{Viewing angle.} The input is one ray per pixel on a common $\log\tau_{\rm LOS}$ axis (contract in the README). With
\code{geometry = "inclined"} the nodes of a ray sit at $x=\xi+t_xz$, $y=\eta+t_yz$, $t=\tan\theta(\cos\phi,\sin\phi)$, and the
horizontal derivatives at fixed height carry the corresponding shear terms; \code{plane\_parallel} keeps every column vertical
with $\tau_{\rm LOS}=\tau/\mu$. $\mu=1$ is exactly the vertical code path.

Option checks in \code{prepare\_embedded}, identical on all ranks: the cut inside the axis, $k_c\ge2$, at least 3 solved
levels, and the ranges of every option in the table. The \code{SlabFFT} plan is built once and reused by every solve.

\section{State, heights and residuals (\code{evaluate!})}
Nodes $m=1\ldots n_1$ are levels $k=k_c\ldots n_\tau$. The unknown vector per column has $n_1$ slots: slot 1 $=c$, slots
$2\ldots n_1=u$ at $k>k_c$ (one array $(n_1,N_y,n_x)$, so FFTs and halos treat it uniformly). At each node the EOS gives
$\ln\rho$, $\partial\ln\rho/\partial\ln P$ and $\ln\chi$; $\partial\ln\chi/\partial\ln P$ by a central difference
($\varepsilon=10^{-4}$, the table returns no opacity derivative). With $a=\tau/\chi$, $q=\rho/P$:
\[
z_{k+1}=z_k-\tfrac12\Delta\ln\tau_k\,(a_k+a_{k+1}),\qquad z_{k_c}=c .
\]
Lorentz force at all levels (\code{lorentz\_tau!}): for each component $f$ of $\B$,
$\partial_zf=(f_{k-1}-f_{k+1})/(z_{k-1}-z_{k+1})$ (one-sided at the ends), $\partial_xf|_z=D_xf-D_xz\,\partial_zf$, likewise
$y$; $\J=\nabla\times\B/\mu_0$; $\Lv=\J\times\B$. Residuals with $W=gq-L_ze^{-u}$, $w=Wa$:
\[
R_x=-D_xu-W\,D_xz+L_xe^{-u},\qquad R_{z,f}=u_{f+1}-u_f-\tfrac12\Delta\ln\tau_f\,(w_f+w_{f+1}) .
\]
Weights from the start state (fixed): $\bar H_m$ global median of $1/(gq)$, $\overline{\Delta z}_f$ global median of the level
thickness, $w_c=\overline{\Delta z}_{\rm node}\bar H^2$, $w_f=\bar H_f\bar H_{f+1}/\overline{\Delta z}_f$;
$\Phi=\sum w_c(R_x^2+R_y^2)+\sum w_fR_z^2$.

\section{Jacobian}
Exact, including $\Lv$'s dependence on the heights (\code{Jop!}, adjoint \code{JTop!}):
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Triangular operator.} $\delta z_m=\delta c+\sum_{f<m}\tfrac12\Delta\ln\tau_f\,(a\chi_u)_f\delta u_f+(a\chi_u)_{f+1}\delta u_{f+1}$ with
$\chi_u=\partial\ln\chi/\partial\ln P$: a cumulative sum down each column. Its adjoint is a reverse cumulative sum.
\item Vertical: $\delta R_{z,f}=\delta u_{f+1}-\delta u_f-\tfrac12\Delta\ln\tau_f(w'_f\delta u_f+w'_{f+1}\delta u_{f+1})$,
$w'=\partial w/\partial u=W'a-Wa\chi_u$, $W'=gq(\rho_u-1)+L_ze^{-u}$.
\item Horizontal: $\delta R_x=-D_x\delta u-W\,D_x\delta z+\ell_x\delta u$, $\ell_x=-D_xz\,W'-L_xe^{-u}$.
\item \textbf{Lorentz through the heights.} With $\zeta_m=(\delta z_{m-1}-\delta z_{m+1})/(z_{m-1}-z_{m+1})$ (the relative change of
the vertical spacing; $\delta z$ above the cut $=\delta c$): $\delta(\partial_zf)=-\partial_zf\,\zeta$,
$\delta(\partial_xf|_z)=-\partial_zf\,D_x\delta z+D_xz\,\partial_zf\,\zeta$. Hence
$\delta\J=\J_\zeta\zeta+\J_XD_x\delta z+\J_YD_y\delta z$ with
$\J_\zeta=(z_yB_{z,z}+B_{y,z},\,-B_{x,z}-z_xB_{z,z},\,z_xB_{y,z}-z_yB_{x,z})/\mu_0$, $\J_X=(0,B_{z,z},-B_{y,z})/\mu_0$,
$\J_Y=(-B_{z,z},0,B_{x,z})/\mu_0$, and $\delta\Lv=\delta\J\times\B$. It enters $\delta R_x$ through $e^{-u}(\delta L_x+z_x\delta L_z)$
and $\delta R_z$ through $e^{-u}a\,\delta L_z$: nine node-local coefficient arrays.
\item Checks (\code{MHS\_CHECK} in the script version): adjoint $\langle Jv,r\rangle=\langle v,J^\top r\rangle$ to $4\times10^{-16}$;
$Jv$ against central differences of $\R$ to $10^{-9}$ (with $\B$), $10^{-10}$ (without). With $\Lv$ held fixed the
directional error was 100\,\% in magnetic regions and the solve stalled after one step.
\end{itemize}

\section{Linear solve and preconditioner}\label{sec:prec}
Normal equations $(J^\top WJ+E+\mu D)\,\delta=-J^\top W\R$ by PCG with global inner products; $E=10^{-2}\kappa^2D$,
$D=(\sum w_c\bar W^2,\ w_{c,2},\dots)$ (the damping shape; slot 1 for $c$, which no vertical equation sees). \code{damping =
"marquardt"} replaces $\mu D$ by $\mu\,{\rm diag}(J^\top WJ)$ (with the block preconditioners). CG accuracy: \code{forcing =
"fixed"} (\code{cg\_tol}, \code{cg\_maxit}) or \code{"ew"} (Eisenstat--Walker forcing: the relative CG tolerance $\eta$ adapted per
step within [\code{eta\_min}, \code{eta\_max}], at most \code{cg\_cap} iterations). Reaching the CG cap does not stop
the outer iteration.

\textbf{\code{precond = "fourier"}} (\code{setprec!}, \code{Minv!}): the operator with layer-median coefficients
($\overline{a\chi_u}$, $\overline{w'}$, $\bar W$) is, per horizontal mode, $M(s^2)=A+s^2B$ with $s^2$ as in
\code{mhs-poisson-z}; $A=G_z^\top W_fG_z+E+\mu D$ (vertical rows), $B=G_h^\top W_cG_h$, $G_h=-(I+\bar W\,S)$ the
horizontal symbol divided by $ik$ including the triangular matrix $S$. Both are dense $n_1\times n_1$. Once per
step: Cholesky $A=LL^\top$, eigen-decomposition $L^{-1}BL^{-\top}=V\Lambda V^\top$, $Q=V^\top L^{-1}$; then
$M(s^2)^{-1}=Q^\top\,{\rm diag}(1/(1+s^2\lambda))\,Q$ for every mode: dense products over all local modes of the transposed FFT
layout (\code{SlabFFT}), in column chunks over the threads.

\textbf{\code{"block"}}: block Jacobi with the exact column blocks of $J^\top WJ$ (Cholesky per column, $n_1\times n_1$). It
captures each column's own coupling (strong-field columns), but cannot propagate the large horizontal scales.
\textbf{\code{"hybrid"}}: the blocks plus the Fourier part on the modes with $|k|\le$ \code{klow} only. \textbf{\code{"switch"}}:
Fourier until the first step whose CG ends at its iteration limit, column blocks after (\code{stats["work"]["switch\_step"]}); on
the Bifrost cube 1.9--4$\times$ faster for the same 25-step budget, about $+1.5$\,GB per rank. A warm start restarts this policy.

\section{Steps}
Levenberg--Marquardt: for $\mu$ (start \code{lm\_mu0}) solve, scale the \emph{whole} step by
$s_c=\min(1,\ $\code{du\_cap}$/\max|\delta u|,\ $\code{dc\_cap\_m}$/\max|\delta c|)$, and backtrack on the true $\Phi$ (EOS
status checked; a trial with cells outside the table backtracks further); if none decreases $\Phi$, $\mu\leftarrow\max(4\mu,1)$
and re-solve (up to 8 times). Damping update after acceptance: \code{lm = "alpha"} (the earlier rule: $\mu/3$ after a full step
$\alpha=1$, unchanged if $\alpha\ge s_c/2$, $\times2$ otherwise, 0 below $10^{-3}$); \code{"gain"} (gain ratio of actual to model
decrease, model $2\alpha g\cdot\delta-\alpha^2|J\delta|_W^2$); \code{"fixed"}. Element-wise clamping of $\delta c$ (the first
version) turned the direction into an ascent direction where the step asked for $>1$\,Mm in a few columns, hence the whole-step
scaling.

\section{Stopping, status and the decrement}\label{sec:stop}
\textbf{\code{stop = "gn"}} (default): the predicted relative decrease of a Gauss--Newton step falls below \code{gn\_tol} on
\code{gn\_consec} consecutive steps; the stop is then \textbf{qualified} at the returned state: an interval $[L,U]$ for the
regularised Gauss--Newton decrement is computed (solved with the column-block preconditioner, at most \code{qual\_maxit} CG
iterations) and must lie below \code{gn\_tol}; an undecided qualification ends \code{unresolved}. \textbf{\code{stop = "phi"}}: the
earlier rule, $\Delta\Phi/\Phi<$ \code{rtol} on \code{n\_consec} steps. Both end at \code{max\_steps} (\code{max\_iter}), when no
damping level gives a decrease (\code{no\_descent}, or \code{domain\_limited} at the table edge), or when the state is at
round-off (\code{phi\_floor}: each residual's terms replaced by their magnitudes times machine precision).

The status (embedding: \code{EquilibriumStatus}; file: attributes \code{status}, \code{stop\_reason}, \code{qual\_outcome},
\code{qual\_lower}, \code{qual\_upper}, \code{gn\_decrement}, \code{gn\_decrement\_source}, \code{roundoff}, \code{phi\_floor},
\code{work\_*}, \code{solve\_seconds}, \code{ranks}, \code{threads}) says which. \code{gn\_decrement} is always an estimate
($b^\top x/\Phi$ of an inexact solve), and \code{gn\_decrement\_source} says from where (the qualification, a converged or capped
final solve, or \code{not\_evaluated}). Exit status of \code{scripts/run.jl}: 0 converged, 2 valid but not converged
(\code{max\_iter}, \code{stalled}), 3 invalid result (no output written), 4 invalid input. A met rule does not certify small
residuals; the logged $\max|\delta c|$ (the largest change of a column's cut-level height) and $z(\tau{=}1)$ are diagnostics only.

The field values on the $\tau$ levels are used unchanged at the new heights; no discrete $\nabla\cdot\B=0$ is imposed (the
staggered construction of \code{full-mhs-z} needs a fixed geometry). Above the cut the levels stay at their column-HSE
pressures and relative heights and move rigidly with $c$; the global height offset is fixed only by the final gauge. The solve
is \emph{step-path sensitive}: on a $24\times18$ crop the same one-rank solve changes by O(1) in a few strong-field column
heights between 1 and 4 BLAS threads (different accepted steps), while $\Phi/\Phi_0$ agrees to $10^{-4}$.

\section{Design history}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Fixed point (\code{scripts/poisson/poisson\_tau.jl}): map to a height grid, Poisson solve there, re-integrate the
$\tau$ columns, repeat. Each pass is a full Poisson solve (1\,h on the full cube), and it diverged where $\Lv$ carries the
gas: the heights were integrated with a pressure that was then replaced. A corrected variant (heights integrated with the Poisson
pressure) settled but lifted the strong-field columns instead of depressing them (median $+400$\,km above the cube mean for
1500--3000\,G).
\item Direct solve with $\Lv$ fixed in the Jacobian: exact without $\B$, stalled in magnetic regions.
\item Exact Jacobian + LM with whole-step scaling + the consecutive-step stop rule: the present mode.
\end{enumerate}
