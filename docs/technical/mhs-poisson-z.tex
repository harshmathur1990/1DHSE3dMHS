\section{Running it}
{\footnotesize\begin{verbatim}
[mpiexec -n R] julia --project=. -t T scripts/run.jl config/modes/mhs-poisson-z.toml
\end{verbatim}}
Driver \code{run\_mhs\_poisson\_z} (\code{src/RunModes.jl}): read, \code{prepare\_embedded(COMM\_WORLD,
:mhs\_poisson\_z, \dots)}, \code{solve\_embedded!}, write, \code{free\_embedded!} (\code{src/Embedded.jl}); solver
\code{module PoissonZ} (\code{src/PoissonZ.jl}); MPI layer \code{src/Slab.jl}. The same solve is available in memory through
the embedded API (\code{PoissonZOptions}; see the \code{column-hse-ltau} notes for the protocol). The input and the \code{[column\_hse]}, \code{[tau]} keys are those of
\code{column-hse-z}; in addition:

\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{L{4.8cm} L{3.4cm} Y}
\toprule
Key & Default & Meaning \\ \midrule
\code{poisson.z\_cut\_m} & $10^6$ & solved region $z\le z_{\rm cut}$; column HSE above \\
\code{poisson.max\_steps} & 100 & Gauss--Newton steps \\

\code{poisson.cg\_tol}, \code{cg\_maxit} & $10^{-3}$, 80 & PCG relative residual and iteration cap per step \\

\end{tabularx}

\section{Flow}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \code{open\_input}; \code{prepare\_embedded} checks the axes ($z$ strictly decreasing, $x$, $y$ uniform to
$10^{-9}$), the options (a cut below the grid or leaving fewer than 3 solved levels is \code{InvalidInput}) and $\ge2$ $x$
columns per rank on all ranks, then builds the $x$-slab context and the \code{SlabFFT} plan once. \code{read\_field} of
$T$, $\B$ on the slab; \code{column\_z!}: the \code{column-hse-z} kernels (\code{hse\_column!}, \code{column\_state!})
per slab column give $P_0$, bitwise equal to \code{column\_hse} on 2-D blocks. If any column leaves the EOS table the
status becomes \code{:eos\_out\_of\_envelope} and the Poisson step is skipped.
\item $\B$ read; all fields converted to $(z,y,x)$ slab arrays; \code{PoissonZ.lorentz}: $\J=\nabla\times\B/\mu_0$ with
centred periodic $x,y$ differences (halo exchange in $x$) and \code{np.gradient}-type $z$ differences, $\Lv=\J\times\B$
at cell centres, fixed.
\item \code{solve\_pressure}: Gauss--Newton on the layers $z\le z_{\rm cut}$ (below).
\item Above $z_{\rm cut}$: per column, trapezoidal hydrostatic step upward, $u_k-u_{k+1}+\tfrac{\Delta z\,g}{2}(q_k+q_{k+1})=0$
($q=\rho/P$), solved by Newton with $\partial q/\partial u=q(\partial\ln\rho/\partial\ln P-1)$.
\item \code{height\_state!}: $\rho$, $n_e$, $\chi_{500}$, EOS flags, $\tau_{500}$ (\code{exp\_tail}), $z(\tau{=}1)$; validity
checks; cooperative output.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Discretisation and objective}
Unknown $u=\ln P$ on the $n$ solved layers. With $e=e^{-u}$, $q=\rho/P$ from the EOS:
\begin{gather*}
R_x=-D_xu+L_xe,\qquad R_y=-D_yu+L_ye\qquad(\text{centres}),\\
R_{z,f}=-\frac{u_f-u_{f+1}}{h_f}-g\frac{q_f+q_{f+1}}2+\frac{L_{z,f}e_f+L_{z,f+1}e_{f+1}}2\qquad(\text{interior faces}),
\end{gather*}
$D_x,D_y$ centred periodic. $\Phi=\sum w_k\bar H_k^2(R_x^2+R_y^2)+\sum h_f\bar H_f^2R_{z,f}^2$, $w$ cell heights,
$\bar H_k$ the global layer median of $1/(gq)$ in the start state, $\bar H_f=\sqrt{\bar H_k\bar H_{k+1}}$ (weights fixed).

\section{Gauss--Newton step}
Linearisation: $\delta R_x=-D_x\delta u-L_xe\,\delta u$ (same for $y$),
$\delta R_{z,f}=-(a_f\delta u_f+b_f\delta u_{f+1})$ with
$a_f=1/h_f+\tfrac12(g\,q'_f+L_{z,f}e_f)$, $b_f=-1/h_f+\tfrac12(g\,q'_{f+1}+L_{z,f+1}e_{f+1})$, $q'=\partial q/\partial u$.
Normal equations $(J^\top WJ+E+A)\,\delta u=-J^\top W\R$, matrix free (\code{Aop!}, \code{JtW!}):
\begin{itemize}
\item $E=10^{-2}\kappa^2w\bar H^2$ damps the modes centred differences cannot see (checkerboard), $\kappa=2\pi/L_x$;
\item anchor $A=A_{\rm anc}\,\mathbf c\mathbf c^\top$, $\mathbf c$ the indicator of the bottom plane, a rank-one
\emph{penalty} on the bottom-plane mean of $\delta u$, $A_{\rm anc}=$ \code{anchor\_w}$\cdot w_{f,n-1}/h_{n-1}^2/\sum c$. It acts
on each step's increment only ($\Phi$ has no anchor term), so it damps the change of that mean per step but does not hold it:
measured drift of the bottom-plane mean $\ln P$ from the start $+0.34$ on an unconverged $24\times18$ window (30 steps),
$2.5\times10^{-4}$ on a converged $11\times12$ window. Unlike the gauge of \code{full-mhs-z}, it is not an exact
reparameterisation. Decision of 24 Oct 2026: this is the intended behaviour.
\end{itemize}
Step: PCG to \code{cg\_tol} or \code{cg\_maxit}; $|\delta u|\le2$ per node (clamp); backtracking on $\Phi$ (halving, up to
12 times, with the EOS status checked).

\textbf{Termination.} The iteration ends when (a) the relative decrease $(\Phi_{\rm prev}-\Phi)/\Phi_{\rm prev}$ of accepted
steps is below \code{rtol} on \code{n\_consec} consecutive steps (log line \code{converged: \dots}); (b) no halving gives a
decrease (\code{no decrease along the step \dots; stopping}); or (c) \code{max\_steps} is reached (no message). The reason is returned in the status and written as the output attribute
\code{stop\_reason}. The driver still returns \code{converged}, i.e.\ exit 0, in all three cases: \textbf{exit 0 is not
evidence of convergence}; read \code{stop\_reason} (code follow-up: propagate the termination into the exit status). Even
case (a) does not certify a small force residual.

\textbf{Stationarity diagnostic.} After the iteration one more system $(J^\top WJ+E+A)\,\delta=-J^\top W\R$ is solved at the
returned state (diagnostic only): \code{gn\_decrement} $=\delta\cdot(-J^\top W\R)/\Phi$, the relative decrease a further
step would predict, logged and written as an attribute; $\|J^\top W\R\|$ is in the embedded status. \code{rms\_atol}
(embedded API only, default 0) accepts an already balanced start in zero steps.

\textbf{Validity.} The continuation above $z_{\rm cut}$ is checked for non-finite pressures (\code{:continuation\_failed});
then \code{check\_fields!} (EOS flags, finiteness, $P,\rho,\chi>0$, $\tau$ strictly increasing downward). The file driver
does not act on the validity flag: an invalid result (including a skipped Poisson step after a column-HSE failure) is
written and the run exits 0 (code follow-up).

\section{Preconditioner}
The Laplacian part with layer-median coefficients: in horizontal Fourier space, mode $(k_x,k_y)$ with
$s^2=\sin^2(k_x\Delta x)/\Delta x^2+\sin^2(k_y\Delta y)/\Delta y^2$, the vertical tridiagonal system
$\mathrm{diag}(c_k+w_k\bar H_k^2s^2+E_k)$ with off-diagonals from the medians of $a_f,b_f$ (\code{setprec!}, exact global
medians by \code{gmedians}), solved by the Thomas algorithm (\code{thomas!}). The rank-1 anchor acts only on the mean mode
$(0,0)$ and is added there exactly by Sherman--Morrison. Without it, CG stalled at its cap after the anchor was changed
from the bottom plane to an irregular cell set; with it, 9--80 iterations.

\textbf{Distributed FFT} (\code{SlabFFT}): (1) per local $x$ plane a real FFT along $y$ (\code{plan\_rfft} on contiguous
$(n,N_y)$ slices, threaded over $x$); (2) \code{MPI.Alltoallv} transpose from $x$ slabs to slabs of $k_y$ (each rank
sends $(n,\,k_y\in\text{dest},\,x\in\text{own})$ blocks); (3) complex FFT along $x$; the vertical solve then runs on contiguous
columns $T[:,k_x,j_\ell]$, threaded over local $k_y$; the inverse retraces the steps. FFTW's own threading is not used (it
segfaults under Julia 1.13). With one rank the transpose is a local permutation.

\section{Parallelisation and reproducibility}
\begin{itemize}
\item $x$ slabs (\code{slab\_par}); $y$ and $z$ complete on every rank. Halo planes in $x$ for every $D_x$
(\code{sddx!}, \code{MPI.Sendrecv!}); all reductions global (\code{gsum}, \code{gdot}, \code{gnorm}, \code{gmax});
medians exact (\code{gquantiles}, bisection on order-preserving integer keys).
\item Threads in every loop over $x$ (stencils, EOS calls, Thomas solves, CG vector updates via \code{tbcast!}; plain
broadcasts and masked indexing were serial bottlenecks: the anchor's \code{v[anc]} allocated 280\,MB per operator call).
\item Reproducibility: medians and quantiles are exact global order statistics, but sums and dot products are
reduced in a rank-dependent order, so results agree only to rounding, and an iterative solver can amplify that along its
path; identical trajectories for different rank or thread counts are not promised. Measured (manual, not an automated
test): $96^2$ crop, 1 vs 4 ranks, $|\Delta\ln P|\le1.5\times10^{-6}$. Automated (\code{mpi/mpi\_embedded.jl}): converged
$11\times12$ window, 2, 3, 4 ranks vs 1 rank $\le1.7\times10^{-12}$ relative (asserted $10^{-9}$); unconverged $24\times18$ window
(30 steps) up to $8.8\times10^{-6}$, recorded.
\item Threads: Julia threads in every loop; BLAS stays at 1 thread; FFTW plans single-threaded, applied from Julia
threads.
\end{itemize}

\section{Verification and performance}
\begin{itemize}
\item \code{test\_embedded.jl}: stored standalone outputs (pre-refactor drivers, \code{b2401a3}) on two windows of the
production input reproduced to $\le10^{-12}$; the rewired file driver reproduced them bitwise. Reason, GN decrement, zero-step
acceptance of a balanced start and the out-of-envelope status are tested.
\item Against the original script (\code{scripts/poisson/poisson\_step.jl} on the same column-HSE file, $96^2$ crop):
$|\Delta\ln P|\le5.8\times10^{-6}$.
\item Full cube ($504\times504\times177$, $z_{\rm cut}=1$\,Mm, 77 solved layers from $-0.49$ to 0.98\,Mm), 24 Oct 2026,
commit 13666b7 (stop rule and step limit since changed), log \code{out/modes/mhs-poisson-z.log} in the cluster worktree
\code{MHS\_modes}; 8 ranks $\times$ 32 threads on 4 nodes: 4.7\,min including column HSE, 4.1\,GB per rank. Step 1 reduced $\Phi$ by 88\,\% (80 CG iterations, 18\,s), step 2 by
another 28\,\% (49 iterations); the run then used the then step limit (60), at $\Phi/\Phi_0=0.081$ and
$\Delta\Phi/\Phi=1.8\times10^{-4}$ per step with 9--11 CG iterations each.
\item On a magnetic $96^2$ crop $\Phi$ creeps ($\Delta\Phi/\Phi\approx1.5\times10^{-3}$ per step for 100 steps): the
low-$\beta$ pressure inflation described in the physics document.
\end{itemize}
