### 3.4 Data-only magnetic feasibility (§4 of your review)

**Observation transfer.** The input B is **cell-centred** and not discretely divergence-free on either mesh. Measured as |div B| h/|B| on the full-resolution input: p50 2.0e-3 cell-centred, against 4.7e-3 for both staggered readings; maximum 16 vs 34–40.

**Problem solved:** minimize t subject to div B = 0 and |B − Bobs| ≤ tσ.
- Exact divergence-freeness through B = curl A + b̄ ẑ.
- The L_∞ fit is approached by L_p continuation (p = 2 → 128, 400 iterations per stage). Each stage gives a **witness** (an upper bound).
- The lower bound is your 1.92.

| Model | least-squares initializer: t / > 3σ / # > 5σ | witness p = 128: t / > 3σ / # > 5σ |
|---|---|---|
| face, `B_f − I·y` (current) | 11.17 / 0.61 % / 341 | **5.13** / 0.70 % / 47 (worst moves to (33, 14, 88)) |
| centre, `H B_f − y` | 21.1 / 2.25 % / 2660 | 6.55 / 1.61 % / 1140 |

A longer face run (1500 iterations per stage, up to p = 256) is still running. Its result will be added to the report.

**Reading:**
- The 11σ outlier is a **least-squares artifact**, not intrinsic to the data. Under the face model the tail limit is met and the maximum is within 0.13σ of 5. This is not yet a feasibility certificate at t = 5.
- Under the centre model (the proper observation residual for cell-centred data) the witnesses fail both the maximum and the tail. The current face interpolation smooths over a centre-level inconsistency of the input itself.
